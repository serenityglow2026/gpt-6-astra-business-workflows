# GPT-6 Astra Business Workflows

A practical open library of **GPT-6 Astra business workflows, automation templates and reusable examples** for research, marketing, SEO, sales, freelancing and operations.

The goal is to move beyond isolated prompt lists and show how AI can be structured around complete, repeatable business tasks.

> A prompt asks for an answer. A workflow defines the job, inputs, process, boundaries, checks and expected output.

## What's inside

This first release contains **16 practical workflow templates**. Each includes a business objective, inputs, step-by-step process, reusable GPT-6 Astra instruction and human-review checkpoint.

## How to use these templates

1. Pick a workflow close to a real task you already perform.
2. Replace generic inputs with approved files, URLs, data and context.
3. Define what Astra may do and what requires human approval.
4. Specify the output format you need.
5. Test on a low-risk example.
6. Review the result and refine the workflow before repeated use.

## Reusable workflow pattern

```text
ROLE: Act as [role].

OBJECTIVE: Complete [specific business outcome].

INPUTS: Use [approved sources].

PROCESS:
1. [Step one]
2. [Step two]
3. [Step three]
4. Verify [critical checks].

RULES:
- Do not [prohibited action].
- Ask before [consequential action].
- Flag uncertainty rather than guessing.

OUTPUT: Return [required format].

REVIEW: Check accuracy, completeness, sources and formatting.
```

## Why workflows instead of prompt dumps?

Reusable business work usually involves more than generating text. A useful workflow defines where information comes from, what sequence should happen, what the model may change, where approval is required, what verification is needed and what the final deliverable should look like.

## Responsible use

These templates are starting points, not guarantees of accuracy or business results. Review consequential outputs before acting on them. Be especially careful with customer data, finances, contracts, health information, security-sensitive systems and actions that change or delete data.
